# Geographica batch 4: plan (nothing drafted yet)

## What the analytics say (16 Oct, 15 public episodes, all old-style)

- The top six by views are all border or "country that shouldn't exist" stories: doubly-landlocked (3,455), france-brazil-border, gambia-river, kaliningrad-exclave, brazil-borders, dahala-khagrabari.
- Mystery stories lead the rest (median 155 views per day of age against 90), but the sample is small and the gap is not a finding yet. No other feature has enough episodes to rank.
- Nothing from batches 2 and 3 (charts, shading, bleed, loop, tilt) has a view count yet. The first of them go public on 23 Oct, so the comparison starts around 26 Oct.

So batch 4 keeps six slots on the proven family (an odd border or an exclave), each with a new capability on it, and four slots on other shapes, so the next comparison has something to separate.
