# Geographica batches 8 and 9: plan (24 episodes)

Twenty-four episodes, about 12 days of slots. Batch 7 leans on transformations; these two mix the other patterns (mechanic, comparison, profile, mystery) and every device we have (profile, cartogram, flow, satellite, ring, day and night), so the next analytics comparison has more to separate. Each is researched from pinned pages, drafted, and previewed silently before any voice. Standing rules: every figure from a pinned page or computed from pinned data and said to be; no old outline drawn; no cause stated that a source doesn't back; disputed areas kept out of frame or sent to your borders review.
