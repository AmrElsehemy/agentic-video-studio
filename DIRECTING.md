# Viral Director Contract

The studio does not turn records into slides. It turns one arguable idea into a short story.

Every episode must contain:

1. **Tension in frame one** — contradiction, risk, surprise, or a defensible hot take.
2. **An open loop** — a question the viewer cannot answer from the hook alone.
3. **An evidence chain** — at least three escalating proofs, not unrelated facts.
4. **Pattern interrupts** — a meaningful visual, copy, scale, colour, or sound change every 1.2 seconds or less.
5. **A counterpoint** — the claim must survive a real objection.
6. **A payoff** — resolve the opening promise before asking for engagement.
7. **A debatable interaction** — ask viewers to choose between credible positions.

`npm run engagement -- <episode-id>` scores these structural requirements. A score below 80 blocks director approval. This is a pre-render heuristic, not proof of virality. Actual watch-time, hold rate, completion, rewatch, share, save, and comment data must eventually feed the director's next decision.
